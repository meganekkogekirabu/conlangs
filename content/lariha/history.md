title: "Proto-Lariha history"
---

Material culture
================

Early Proto-Larihans are generally associated with the Tamu archaeological
culture of southern Korea, which lasted from around 46000 years before present
to 40000 years BP. The Tamu culture is characterised by cord-marked pottery
similar to that of the Jomon people, but also used unique knapped amygdaloid
microliths, manufactured similarly to the Mousterian industry. Around 40000
years BP, Larihan populations began to migrate eastward and would settle
throughout most of what is now Japan. Around a thousand years afterwards,
Proto-Japonic populations began to displace Larihan populations in the south of
the peninsula. During the recession of the last glacial maximum, Japan became
an archipelago and its inhabitants would not be in contact with mainland Asia
until the chalcolithic. Proto-Larihans around 20000 BP had domesticated
Larihica rice (*Oryza sativa larihica*), which was similar to champa rice in
that it was quickly growing, but it was not as resistant to drought.
Fermentation and mudbricks were also discovered around this time, as shown by
archaeological and linguistic evidence. It is unclear when Proto-Larihan
diverged, but it is likely that the Sanriku and Hokkaido branches were the
first to diverge.
