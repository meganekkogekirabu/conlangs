Morphophonology
===============


Qualitative ablaut
------------------

Affixes can affect the *grade* of the root they attach to, that being the
nucleus of the root. It may be in one of four states:

- full-grade (a)
- reduced-grade (u)
- null-grade (&empty;)
- compensatory-grade (á)

In the null-grade, any consonants which occupy the nucleus will become vowels.
This is done according to these simple rules:

- y &rarr; i
- peripherals &rarr; u
- coronals &rarr; e


Consonant assimilation
----------------------

Proto-Lariha had a system of consonant assimilation whereby, on affixation
to a verb root, the consonants in the affix would have to assimilate to the
adjacent ones inside the root, according to the following rules:

- coronal &ctdot; tense peripheral&emsp;&rarr;&emsp;peripheral &ctdot; tense peripheral
- coronal &ctdot; peripheral&emsp;&rarr;&emsp;velarised coronal &ctdot; peripheral
- peripheral &ctdot; coronal&emsp;&rarr;&emsp;tense peripheral &ctdot; coronal
- peripheral &ctdot; coronal &ctdot; peripheral&emsp;&rarr;&emsp;tense peripheral &ctdot; peripheral &ctdot; /w/

Syntax
======

Relative clauses are chained on in one of three ways:

1. `[PRON VP (NP)] wh NP`
2. `NP th [VP]`
3. `NP [wa VP NP] wh`

The word order is generally SOV but with topic-comment syntax. The topic marker
is *wa* and follows NP.

Proto-Lariha possesses split ergativity: present tense clauses use nominative /
accusative alignment and non-present clauses use ergative / absolutive. The
affixes for the absolutive and accusative cases are identical, however.


Adjectives
==========

Adjectives are always formed from verbs. Adnouns use *-i* and adverbs use *-ang*.
