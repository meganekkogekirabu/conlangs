Positions
=========

Verbs in Proto-Lariha are reconstructed to have had *positions*, similar to,
e.g., Proto-Kartvelian. These positions are represented by the below table.
The positions are numbered based on their relative position to the root: -1 is
directly prefixed to the root, 1 is directly suffixed, 2 follows 1, and 3
follows 2.

| (-1) temporal deixis   | (1) aspect                    | (2) speech-act deixis | (3) modality       |
| :--------------------- | :---------------------------- | :-------------------- | :----------------- |
| *ta-*: pre-state       | *-an*: perfective I           | *-w*: locuphoric      | *-tsa*: infinitive |
| *nu-*: post-state      | *(&empty;)-ká*: perfective II | *-n*: aliophoric      | *-&empty;*: realis |
| *&empty;-*: event time | *(u)-tá*: gnomic              | *-y*: obviative       | *-ang*: irrealis   |
| *ke-*: e-narrative     | *(á)-ts*: stative             |                       |                    |

In addition to perfectives I and II, there is also a perfective III formed by
reduplication of the form C<sub>1</sub>VC<sub>2</sub> &rarr;
C<sub>1</sub>uC<sub>1</sub>áC<sub>2</sub>. Perfective III can also trigger
dissimilation:
